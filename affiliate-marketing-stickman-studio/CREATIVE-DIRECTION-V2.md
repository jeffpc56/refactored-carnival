# REVISED CREATIVE DIRECTION — Poppy vs. the Stickmen

Date: 2026-10-16
Status: Approved directional correction from Jeff; supersedes the old assumption that the stickmen merely illustrate a voiceover.

## The central joke
POPPY IS THE STAR. A consistent AI version of Jeff (long gray hair, gray beard, vest) appears in the foreground, looks at the camera, and SPEAKS the entire teaching narration, with visible mouth movement synchronized to spoken audio.

Behind him is a very large TV or whiteboard. Stickmen live INSIDE this screen and mischievously MOCK POPPY AS HE SPEAKS. They imitate him, exaggerate his examples, misunderstand his teaching and sometimes deliberately misbehave. Poppy occasionally turns toward the TV to scold them, then returns to teaching while they misbehave again.

The stickmen ARE supporting comedy characters, not narrators or merely passive diagrams. Poppy's lessons must remain useful, truthful, simple, and clear.

## Example gag
POPPY: "When I first started affiliate marketing, I thought all I needed was a link and some traffic."
SCREEN: A stickman dressed like Poppy holds a huge affiliate link and chases other stickmen around the TV.
POPPY turns: "HEY. THAT IS NOT WHAT I MEANT."
SCREEN: Stickman freezes, hides the link, and whistles. Poppy returns to camera. Stickman resumes chasing people.

## Direction rules
1. Poppy is the only regular speaking character. Spoken narration belongs to him; lip sync is essential.
2. Stickmen are silent visual hecklers, pantomiming and mocking the words in real time.
3. Leave room in narration for visual reactions. Every gag has setup, stickman mockery, Poppy reaction and return to lesson.
4. Maintain consistent Poppy face/wardrobe/studio across shots.
5. Camera should frame Poppy and screen together whenever possible. When Poppy turns, his gaze and reactions should clearly point toward the TV.
6. Stickmen stay visually within screen/whiteboard, with clean high contrast.
7. No exaggerated income claims or fake results. Humor should expose real beginner frustrations.
8. The earlier still-presenter narrated MP4 is a ROUGH PROTOTYPE and does not achieve this corrected vision. It needs a true talking-character/lip-sync workflow for a finished version.
