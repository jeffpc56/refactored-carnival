# Permanent Studio Layout Rule

Approved October 16, 2026.

The LARGE TV OR WHITEBOARD MUST ALWAYS BE IN THE BACKGROUND, BEHIND POPPY.

- Poppy stands/sits in the foreground, speaks to camera, and has realistic synchronized mouth motion.
- The TV/whiteboard is physically behind him, not side-by-side as a separate split-screen graphic or floating overlay in the foreground.
- Frame a wide camera angle that clearly shows Poppy and the whole usable drawing surface together.
- Mischievous stickmen appear on the background TV/whiteboard, silently mocking his commentary.
- Poppy sometimes turns to catch them; they freeze, then resume when he faces camera.
- Keep the same spatial relationship and consistent studio setup in every shot.
- Prefer a background screen offset enough to be visible and not blocked by Poppy's body.
- When generating images or footage, preserve foreground/background depth, lighting and screen perspective.
- Do not replace Poppy's speaking role with an offscreen narrator.

This is an explicit, non-negotiable composition constraint for the series.
