# Affiliate Marketing Stickman Studio

Created: October 16, 2026
Producer: Jeff C / Poppy AI Studio
Status: Concept and Episode 1 script drafted. No audio or finished video generated yet.

## Core concept
Blend two video styles:
1. https://github.com/ai-playbooks/videoexpress-consistent-character-talking-video
2. https://github.com/ai-playbooks/videoexpress-stickman-story-agent

An engaging, consistent talking presenter stands in a studio. A giant TV or whiteboard behind the presenter plays short, expressive stick-figure animations that illustrate each lesson. Sometimes the presenter reacts to the stickman. The presenter and drawings must occupy separate layers so timing and continuity can be edited. These are creative references, not a claim that the workflows automatically integrate.

## Style
- Original, truthful, funny, useful, calm, non-hype.
- Speak to new or frustrated online affiliate marketers.
- Avoid exaggerated income claims, guaranteed results, and fake statistics.
- Character option: presenter with long gray hair, beard and casual vest, inspired by Poppy. Final likeness not yet decided.
- Consistent studio layout, bright readable TV screen, 16:9 landscape.
- Conversational voice with small pauses for humor; one stable voice across episodes.
- Use a clear teaching point in every gag.
- Closing philosophy: Start Simple. Help Somebody. Track What Happens. Give It Time.
- Keep episodes modular in short separate shots for editing and voice synchronization.

## Episode 1: How To Survive Your First Affiliate Marketing Campaign
Working series name: THE AFFILIATE MARKETING CIRCUS.
Target: ~90–120 seconds, subject to final voice pacing.

### Scene 1: Welcome
PRESENTER: "Welcome to affiliate marketing. The wonderful world where you can work from home, be your own boss, and spend six hours trying to figure out why nobody clicked your link."
SCREEN: Stickman at laptop. Clock spins. Analytics: ZERO CLICKS. Refresh. ZERO CLICKS. He puts on glasses.
PRESENTER: "Yep. We have all been there."

### Scene 2: The Magic Link
PRESENTER: "Somebody tells you all you need is an affiliate link and some traffic. Sounds easy enough."
SCREEN: Stickman throws a giant affiliate link toward a crowd. They run away. One shrugs.
PRESENTER: "Well, that went over like a screen door on a submarine."

### Scene 3: Shiny Objects
PRESENTER: "So what do we do when our first campaign does not work. Naturally, we buy another tool."
SCREEN: Stickman buys one shiny tool, then several. Boxes stack until only feet show.
PRESENTER: "Congratulations. You now have seventeen tools, four subscriptions, and absolutely no idea what you were originally trying to accomplish."

### Scene 4: Find The Right Person
PRESENTER: "But here is where things start making sense. Affiliate marketing is not about throwing links at strangers. It is about finding people who have a problem and helping them discover something useful."
SCREEN: Stickman holds sign NEED MORE TRAFFIC. Another holds I NEED HELP GETTING VISITORS. They meet.
PRESENTER: "Now we are getting somewhere."

### Scene 5: Keep It Simple
PRESENTER: "You do not need fifty tools to start. You need a clear message. A useful offer. A way to follow up with interested people. And a way to track what happens."
SCREEN: Stickman draws AD -> LANDING PAGE -> SIGNUP -> FOLLOW-UP. Then adds seventeen useless arrows.
PRESENTER, turning toward TV: "HEY. KEEP IT SIMPLE."
SCREEN: Stickman erases extra arrows.

### Scene 6: The Lesson
PRESENTER: "Here is something worth remembering. One click starts a visit. One signup starts a relationship. And one good relationship can be worth more than a thousand people who were never interested."
SCREEN: Stickman stops chasing crowds and helps one person.
PRESENTER: "Start simple. Help somebody. Track what happens. And give your campaign enough time to learn something."

### Final gag
SCREEN: Stickman raises I GOT MY FIRST SIGNUP. Confetti. He checks email. Subscriber has already unsubscribed.
PRESENTER: "Welcome to affiliate marketing, buddy. Now keep learning."
END CARD: THE BEST MARKETING TOOL IS THE ONE YOU ACTUALLY USE. / Poppy AI Studio.

## Production plan
1. Lock presenter visual reference and studio with large TV/whiteboard.
2. Record or synthesize voiceover in separate lines per scene with consistent voice; license/permission for cloned voices is required.
3. Generate seven white-background stickman animation clips, one per section; consistent stroke weight and character.
4. Generate talking-presenter shots matching voice and narration.
5. Composite animated clips inside TV/whiteboard with perspective, reflections, and appropriate masking; keep TV visuals readable.
6. Insert jokes as quick cuts, with presenter reaction beats. Add captions and modest sound effects.
7. Check factual claims and confirm animation, voice and presenter do not drift between shots.
8. Export 1920x1080 MP4, plus captions/transcript. No exported media exists yet.
