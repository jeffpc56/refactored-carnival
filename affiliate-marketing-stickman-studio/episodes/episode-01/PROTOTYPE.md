## Narration (exact prototype)
### 1. Welcome to Affiliate Marketing
Welcome to affiliate marketing. Where you can work from home, be your own boss, and spend six hours trying to figure out why nobody clicked your link. Yep. We have all been there.

### 2. The Magic Affiliate Link
Somebody tells you all you need is an affiliate link and some traffic. Sounds easy enough. So you throw your link at everyone. Well, that went over like a screen door on a submarine.

### 3. The Shiny Object Trap
So what do we do when our first campaign does not work? Naturally, we buy another tool. Congratulations! You now have seventeen tools, four subscriptions, and no idea what you were originally trying to accomplish.

### 4. Find the Right Person
But here is where things start making sense. Affiliate marketing is not about throwing links at strangers. It is about finding people who have a problem, and helping them discover something useful.

### 5. Keep It Simple
You do not need fifty tools to start. You need a clear message, a useful offer, a way to follow up with interested people, and a way to track what happens. Hey! Keep it simple!

### 6. One Signup. One Relationship.
One click starts a visit. One signup starts a relationship. And one good relationship can be worth more than a thousand people who were never interested. Start simple. Help somebody. Track what happens. And give your campaign time to teach you something.

### 7. Welcome to the Circus
Our stickman finally got his first signup! Oh. And the subscriber already unsubscribed. Welcome to affiliate marketing, buddy. Keep learning. The best marketing tool is the one you actually use.
